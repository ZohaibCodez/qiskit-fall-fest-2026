## What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of student-run quantum computing events, organized locally by university chapters and community groups under IBM's Qiskit program. Each fest brings talks, hands-on workshops, and community activities to introduce people to quantum computing using Qiskit — IBM's open-source quantum SDK.

## New to quantum computing?

Quantum computing uses the principles of quantum mechanics — superposition and entanglement — to process information in ways classical computers can't. You don't need a physics or math background to get started: Qiskit lets you build and run quantum circuits with Python, and our sessions are designed to meet beginners where they are.

## This year's theme: A Decade of Quantum on the Cloud

2026 marks ten years since quantum computers first became accessible over the cloud. This year's Fall Fest looks back at how far the field has come — and forward at what's next — through talks, workshops, and hands-on sessions built around cloud-accessible quantum hardware.

## Who this event is for

Students, developers, researchers, and anyone curious about quantum computing — no prior experience required. Whether you're attending your first tech event or you've already written a few quantum circuits, there's a track for you.

## What you'll gain

- A working understanding of core quantum computing concepts
- Hands-on experience writing and running Qiskit circuits
- Exposure to real quantum hardware via the IBM Quantum Platform
- Connections with a local community of quantum-curious peers
